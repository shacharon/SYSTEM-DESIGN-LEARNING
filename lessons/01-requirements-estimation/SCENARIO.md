# Lesson 01 — Scenario: LinkLite, a URL shortener (fictional)

You are the lead engineer at a small startup, **LinkLite**. Marketing teams paste a long web address and receive a short link such as `lnk.example/Ab3xYz`. When anyone opens the short link, they are redirected to the original address.

What the business tells you:
- The company expects **about 100 million new short links per month** after launch.
- Short links are opened **about 100 times for every link created**.
- Links should keep working for **5 years** unless the owner deletes them.
- Marketing teams care a lot about **clicks being counted** (a simple total per link is enough for now).
- A redirect that feels slow will make customers complain.

Before sizing anything, you want to be sure you are solving the right problem.

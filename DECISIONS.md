# DECISIONS — Architecture decision records (learner-owned)

Used from Module 7 (lesson 29 onward). The **learner writes** each record; the mentor reviews it with the rubric in `MENTOR.md` section 8 and never rewrites it without approval. Add entries at the end; never rewrite history, supersede instead.

**Confidentiality:** records are about fictional or sanitised scenarios. No real company, product, client, colleague, credential or confidential detail.

Entry template (same as `CLAUDE-AI-AGENTIC-BASIC/banking-agent/learning/DECISIONS.md`):

```
### D<number>: <short title>
- Date:
- Module:
- Status: proposed | approved | superseded by D<n>
- Context: the requirement and constraints
- Options considered: at least two, with trade-offs
- Decision: what was chosen
- Why: the reasons, including what was rejected
- Consequences: risks accepted, what to revisit, how it will be verified
```

# STORIES — STAR story bank (learner-owned)

Used from Module 8 (lesson 33 onward). The **learner writes every story from real experience**; the mentor reviews and asks follow-ups but never writes, invents or embellishes a story.

**Confidentiality (required):** sanitise before saving. No employer, client, product or colleague names, no confidential figures, no customer or personal data, no secrets. Describe the situation generically ("a payments team at a mid-size company"). If a detail could identify a real company or person, remove it.

Competency tags: conflict · failure · leadership · ownership · ambiguity · mentoring · influence-without-authority.

Entry template:

```
### S<n>: <short title>
- Competencies:
- Situation:
- Task:
- Action: (what I did and decided; "I", not "we")
- Result: (measurable)
- Reflection: (what I would do differently)
- Sanitised? yes
- Rating history: (date, weak|ok|strong)
```
